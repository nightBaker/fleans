---
title: Interface theme
description: Switch the management UI between the default Orbit look and the BCC Hub brand theme.
---

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the side navigation and pick a **UI Theme**:

- **Orbit** (default) — the standard Fleans look. **Accent Color** recolours it.
- **BCC Hub** — the BCC HUB brand: Purple accent, Light Grey and white surfaces, black text, the
  *bcc hub* logo in the side navigation and the top bar, and labels in standard case instead of
  capitals. Buttons, eyebrows and labels use the brand's mono font, Formular Neutral Mono, if it is
  installed on your computer; otherwise JetBrains Mono. The brand fixes the accent colour, so
  **Accent Color** is disabled while this theme is active.

The theme applies at once and to every page, including the BPMN editor and the instance diagram when
**Diagram Style** is *Orbit*. **Theme Mode** (System / Light / Dark) and the sun/moon button still
switch between the light and dark variants of either theme.

The choice is saved in your browser's local storage, so it survives reloads but not a different browser
or a private window. If browser storage is unavailable, Settings shows an error and the theme lasts
only until the page is reloaded.
